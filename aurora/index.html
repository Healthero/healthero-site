The line

Five steps. The first is free.

You run Sense, Shape and Build yourself with Aurora. Guide and Govern are an engagement with Healthero, for teams ready to put what they built to work.

  1. 01Free

    Sense

    Chat with Aurora to find the real problem. It asks what a good colleague would: who is affected, what you have seen, what you are assuming.

    You leave with: a problem statement specific to healthcare, and a strategic assessment of your idea.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Name what has to change and where the leverage is. Build the worst-day scenario your idea must survive. Those scenarios become the requirements.

    You leave with: one idea written as a test: if we do this, that happens, for these people.

  3. 03

    Build

    Make the smallest version people can try, and put it in front of the first people who need it most. Record what they do, then what they say.

    You leave with: a prototype, feedback on record, and a call to keep, change or stop.

  4. 04Engagement

    Guide

    Align what you built to a living blueprint: a shared, current picture of who does what, which decisions belong to whom, and what the solution must never do.

    You leave with: a living blueprint your team owns, and a build aligned to it.

  5. 05Engagement

    Govern

    Live decision protocols run in the background alongside your people: which decisions stay human, what agents may propose, and what is logged when someone overrides.

    You leave with: decision protocols running with one team, a record of overrides, and a review rhythm.

Made-up example · Strategic assessment

Night-shift discharge teaching

The problem
Families who are not in the room at a night discharge go home with instructions they have not been taught, and some come back.
Confidence
40%. Two of five notes were observed on the unit. Three are assumptions.
Check these first
That return visits are tied to night discharges. That families want a call the next morning. That the interpreter line is the bottleneck.
Next step
Sit with three families at tomorrow's discharges and write down what they ask.

For product teams

Know your answer before a buyer asks who decides

Aurora takes a team from a hunch to a prototype people have tried, a decision you can defend, and a clear account of what still rests on a guess. It is the free way into the decision design work at healthero.io. Teams coming out of a hackathon or an accelerator can start here too.

The team behind each step

Agents do the legwork. You sign every step.

Each step has its own specialist agents and one rule they cannot break. Nothing moves to the next step until a person on your team has reviewed it.

Specialist agents and guardrails by step
StepSpecialist agentsGuardrail
Sense Researcher, synthetic persona No patient details. A synthetic persona never counts as evidence.
Shape Ideation facilitator, scenario writer, jobs catalog You answer first. Every idea carries its worst day.
Build Experience designer, visual designer, interface developer Accessible components and made-up records only. A person approves before anything is shared.
Guide Blueprint keeper, usability reviewer, manual writer The blueprint changes only when a named person approves the change.
Govern Protocol monitor, evaluator, decision ledger Agents propose. A named person decides, and every override is logged.

Confidence score

How much is seen, how much is guessed

Every note is marked observed or assumed. A step's confidence is the share your team observed. A plan resting on guesses says so on its face.

Trust score

How often the agents earned their keep

Your team rates each reply useful or off. The share rated useful is the trust score. It sets how much the agents may draft, and they never act without your approval.

Why Aurora

  • Judgment. Aurora asks about bias, ethics, culture and who pays before anyone builds.
  • Trust. Every claim shows its source or is marked as an assumption.
  • Skill. You answer first, then Aurora responds. You should need it less each time.

We won't

  • Use real patient data. Prototypes run on made-up records only.
  • Call a prototype a product. It proves the idea, not the build.
  • Promise funding. We promise evidence, including a clear stop.

How Aurora thinks

Signal. Social. Code.

Most problems show up in one place and start in another. Aurora looks at three layers before it suggests anything. Don't automate confusion.

What you can see

Signal

Delays, errors, burnout, tools nobody uses. The friction and outcomes on the surface.

How people work together

Social

Workflows, trust, incentives, workarounds. This is where a prototype belongs first.

What makes it the default

Code

Policies, data, rules, automation. The hardest layer to change, so it changes last.
